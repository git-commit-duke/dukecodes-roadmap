# Certs

Each cert comes right after the milestone that teaches it, so study time doubles as lab time. Re-check the price and exam version on the official page before you book: they change.

**Status** is one of `planned`, `optional`, `later`, `studying`, `booked`, `passed`, `expired` or `skipped`. A `passed` cert needs a ledger row with the badge link. **Label** is the weakest claim in the row: `FACT` (read on the official page on the Checked date), `COMMUNITY` (from forums or resellers, not the official page), `UNVERIFIED` (not checked).

## Choices to make

- **Security+ version.** SY0-701 has more study material today. SY0-801 is current for longer. Studying is under way, so pick the version before booking.
- **LFCS.** Take it only if the M2 reps feel hard.
- **GitHub certs.** GH-900 is a quick win after M0 and never needs renewal. GH-200 and GH-500 fit after M6, where you build and harden a pipeline. Book GH-300 only after a few months of daily Copilot use. GH-100 and GH-600 are optional. GH-700 (Agentic AI Business Professional, in beta) is for business roles, so it is not listed. Register with a personal Microsoft account, not a work one: exam records stay with the account. A verified GitHub student gets one free GH-900 or GH-300 voucher.
