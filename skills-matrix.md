# Skills matrix

Rate yourself honestly, then re-rate at the end of every milestone. The numbers matter less than the trend and the evidence.

**Score:** `?` not rated yet · `0` never done it · `1` done it with a guide open · `2` done it alone and can explain why it works · `3` could teach it, or fix it when it breaks under pressure.

A score of 2 or 3 needs **Evidence**: a link to a file in this repo, a ledger row (`ledger.csv`), or an https link to your work. `roadmap check` fails a 2 or 3 without one.
