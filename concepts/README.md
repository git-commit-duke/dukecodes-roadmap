# Concept explainers

Each milestone's **Explain it** list names three concepts. Write each one here as `concepts/<slug>.md`, using [the template](../templates/concept.md). The showcase page tracks each one as to write, written or reviewed.

## The rules

- **First principles.** Start from the problem, not the tool. "Why does this exist?" comes before "how do I configure it?".
- **Under 500 words.** If it won't fit, you're explaining two concepts.
- **Plain English.** A friend with no tech background should follow it. Define jargon or link the [glossary](../glossary.md).
- **Tested.** Every explainer says what you ran or broke to check your understanding.
- **Reviewed.** Ask someone to poke holes in it: a peer, a mentor, or an AI reviewer told to find mistakes. Fix what they find, then add `**Reviewed:** YYYY-MM-DD · who · what changed`.
- **Your words.** A reviewer can question and correct. The writing is yours, or it doesn't count.

## Practise out loud

The showcase page has a drill mode: it picks a random concept question and gives you two minutes to answer out loud. Interviews test exactly this.
